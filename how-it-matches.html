How an answer becomes an area.

Every choice in the quiz nudges eight underlying traits. After ten questions those traits are rescaled and measured against twelve fixed London-area profiles — whichever area sits closest wins. The flow below is the mechanism; the tables beneath it are the actual numbers, so you can see — and push back on — exactly what's driving each result.
